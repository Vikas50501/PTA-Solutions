Diagnostic Procedures

Explore Hearing Care

Clinical diagnostic evaluations conducted in our sound-treated testing suite in Chembur, Mumbai.

A

Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA)

PTA is a hearing test that measures the quietest sounds a person can hear at different pitches. The results help assess the degree and pattern of hearing loss and whether hearing sensitivity differs between the ears.

Who may benefit: People who have difficulty hearing conversations, ask others to repeat themselves, or turn up the TV volume.

What to expect: Listening through headphones/earphones; bone-conduction testing when indicated.

Results: Plotted on an audiogram and explained clearly to guide appropriate next steps.

B

Tympanometry

Tympanometry assesses how the eardrum moves in response to changes in air pressure in the ear canal. It helps evaluate the function of the eardrum and middle-ear system.

Who may benefit: People with ear fullness, suspected middle-ear problems, or conductive hearing concerns.

What to expect: A small soft probe placed at the ear canal entrance; quick and painless.

Results: Assesses middle-ear function alongside patient symptoms and test results.

C

Speech Audiometry (When Indicated)

Speech audiometry assesses how well a person detects or recognises spoken words at suitable listening levels. It provides additional information about functional hearing.

Who may benefit: People who hear sounds but struggle to understand speech in noise.

What to expect: Listening to word material and responding according to test procedures.

Results: Interpreted with pure-tone audiometry for a complete listening profile.

D

Otoacoustic Emissions (OAE) Test

OAE testing checks for sound responses produced by the inner ear, particularly the outer hair cells. It is commonly used in newborn hearing screening.

Who may benefit: Newborns, infants and other patients when clinically indicated.

What to expect: A small probe in the ear canal measures the cochlear response painlessly.

Results: Indicates hair cell function; follow-up testing scheduled if indicated.

E

BERA / ABR Hearing Assessment

BERA, also called ABR, is an objective hearing assessment that records electrical responses from the auditory pathway in response to sound.

Who may benefit: Babies, children, or adults requiring objective auditory pathway assessment.

What to expect: Electrodes on the skin record responses while sound is played via earphones.

Results: Interpreted by our clinician to determine hearing thresholds objectively.

F

Hearing-Aid Consultation, Trial & Fitting

We help patients understand their hearing assessment and explore hearing-aid options based on their hearing needs, communication goals, comfort, lifestyle and budget.

What the service includes: Device discussion, trial where available, fitting, daily care and follow-up adjustments.

A personalised approach: Tailored to individual audiograms, ear condition, comfort, and listening needs.
