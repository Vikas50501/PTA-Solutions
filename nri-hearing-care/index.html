NRI & Family Hearing-Care Coordination

You Live Abroad. We Help Coordinate Your Parents' Hearing & Speech Care in Mumbai.

If your parent lives in Mumbai and you live abroad, PTA Solutions can help you understand their hearing or communication needs, explain the assessment findings and plan the next steps. With your parent's informed consent, we can discuss the recommendations with you by WhatsApp or video call.

Clear assessments. Practical recommendations. Care planned around your parent's needs.

How NRI Parent Care Works

How NRI Parent Care Works

01

Tell Us About Your Parent

Contact us and briefly explain your parent's hearing or communication concern. We will help you understand which appointment may be appropriate.

02

Book the Initial Assessment

We confirm the service, venue, fee and what is included before the appointment.

03

Understand the Findings

We explain the assessment results and discuss practical recommendations with your parent.

04

Connect With You Abroad

With your parent's informed consent, we can discuss the findings and next steps with you through a scheduled WhatsApp or video call.

05

Choose the Next Steps

If more care is needed, we provide a separate estimate for additional tests, hearing aids, home visits, therapy or follow-up. You can decide what to proceed with.

Clear Recommendations Before You Commit to Further Care

We explain the assessment findings, available options and expected costs before arranging additional services. You can review the recommendations with your parent and decide how you would like to proceed.
