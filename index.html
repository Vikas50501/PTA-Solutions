COMPREHENSIVE AUDIOLOGY

Clinical Hearing Tests & Tympanometry

schedule Session: 45 Mins

Precision air and bone conduction Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA) inside a sound-treated testing room, coupled with impedance audiometry to measure eardrum compliance and acoustic reflex integrity.

Diagnostic Scope

Full octave threshold plotting (250Hz - 8000Hz) & middle ear fluid analysis

Recommended For

Adults, seniors, musicians & individuals experiencing muffled speech
